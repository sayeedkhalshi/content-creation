## Story Identity

In a control structure, the learner is a user, a community.

People are memory. But the memory is not what we perceive.

When a learner goes through a learning cycle remembers is as a story, when he expresses, that's also a story. That story has always have a perspective.

A memory is saved as a perspective.

Let's think about some dumb person around you, could you check he still has some kind of thinking processed that let him decide for himself. If you go deep, you will see some matters he is thinking from his old childhood and he still analyzes it, still develops upon it. Now look at yourself, you will see the same inside you.

If you look carefully, it's no less than einstein level development in your brain.

This is the main story of you, there are many stories but all shaped to support the main story and enhance it.

That story is your identity, if it's gone, you are gone.

So a learner is a story.

But what is a story? If you look at perspective theory, every story is just from some perspectives (actually one) and its the adventure of the learning cycle.

The story ends when there is a learning philosophy developed, after that a new story starts.

What's more fascinating is, more perspectives there is, more knowledge there is, but if they don't updrade the main flow, single state of action mind fails and we don't have actually meaningful learning that uses.
