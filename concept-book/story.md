## Story

Let’s learn what is story. We all know story is story that tells a story in a social context. What? Come on, we all know what a story is. But are you thinking like a story has a start, end, middle or like a cinema who has 4 act that we got from music that has 4 movements. But in reality, a single line can be a story. There is a underline thing that makes anything a story. That’s flow. You probably never heard of it. It goes towards something. Everything in a story may not have a point but must has to have connection from previous thing to next thing. Which is continuity. For example - if you are going to the moon, you are not fishing. But if you say you are in a rocket going to the moon and using a net to catch fish in the river of your village, that’s a unrealistic false connection. That’s narrative and you have to keep narrative intact and realistic. If you break the flow of any of the properties of stories, you break the story and it will sound absurd. Will take people out of the emotional journey to the norm.

Why? Because our brain stores, processes information following like a story. If the story is broken, means the flow is broken. There are some laws behind flows - It’s hard to get off a flow.
It takes more advance techniques to get off a flow as it becomes a requirement to get off a flow as we grow up. For that same reason we don’t like surprises unless we get used ti surprises.

Like I said, a story can be in a line. Can be in a thought. Thoughts are stories and have flows and narratives, all thoughts connects like a story. If you heard - neurons fired together are wired together. Even neural connections are wired together towards a goal. They are stories. Connections of them are also stories. Breaking off a flow means breaking off the neural connections and it’s a physical thing.
