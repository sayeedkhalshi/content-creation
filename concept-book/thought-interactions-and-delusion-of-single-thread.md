Because of those interactions this simulates the physics universe and flows of it. Creates many more new perspectives, flows, informations or story whatever you feel(name) them.

This allows all learning in any phase can be connected to create a new understanding, cases better upgradation of our learning techniques and philosphies.

Now this also allows us to create 'perceieve less information and think many' habit. I used this to create chain of concepts and better thought generator by connecting each one by each one.
